# Card Survival: Tropical Island

## What does randomization do to this game?

Blueprints are shuffled. Finishing the research of a blueprint sends a check
instead of teaching you that blueprint; blueprints arrive as items, from your own
research or from other players' worlds.

## What is the goal?

Reaching any real ending of the game.

## What stays the same?

Survival itself: hunger, thirst, weather, wounds, exploration, cooking and combat
are unchanged. The nine blueprints every character starts with stay available.
